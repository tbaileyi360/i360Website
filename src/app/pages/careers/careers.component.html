Important: Recruitment scam alert

Unauthorized parties may impersonate i360 recruiters or hiring managers. Fraudulent activity can include fake job postings, interviews, emails, requests for personal information, and links to external portals.

How to verify a real opportunity

  • Official email from an @i360technologies.com address
  • Role referenced on our official careers or LinkedIn presence
  • Live interview with an identifiable i360 employee
  • Never payment requests, gift cards, crypto, or fund transfers
  • Sensitive identity/banking details only via secured HR systems after a written offer

If you were contacted suspiciously

  • Do not reply, click links, or open attachments
  • Do not send money or identity documents
  • Forward details to {{ company.securityEmail }}
  • Report via IC3 and the FTC when appropriate
